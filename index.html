Our Story

The Soul of
The Passenger

Every morning starts with Hausbrandt Gourmet Columbus — premium Italian-Arabic coffee, pulled fresh while Lake Orestiada catches the first light. By noon, the terrace is full and the energy is easy.

After sunset, everything changes. The lights go low, the music goes up, and our bar team takes over with signature cocktails, top-shelf spirits, and an atmosphere that's made The Passenger the place to be in Kastoria after dark.

The Ritual

From Sunrise to Last Call

One space, four distinct moods. The Passenger transforms with the hours.

08:00 — 12:00
The Morning Ritual
Hausbrandt coffee fills the air. Regulars settle in with a freddo espresso and fresh breakfast. Sunlight, good company, and the perfect start to your day.
Hausbrandt Coffee Breakfast Menu Lakeside Calm
12:00 — 18:00
The Afternoon Drift
Cold brews replace hot ones. Friends gather over club sandwiches and sweet waffles. The terrace fills with conversation and the shimmer of the lake below.
Cold Brews Bistro Bites Terrace Dining
18:00 — 22:00
The Golden Transition
The sun drops behind the mountains and the mood shifts. Warm lighting takes over, wines and aperol spritzes come out, and The Passenger starts becoming something different.
Wine Hour Aperitivo Curated Music
22:00 — Late
The Electric Night
This is what The Passenger is known for. The bar lights up with signature cocktails, premium spirits, great music, and the best crowd in town. Kastoria's go-to spot after dark.
Signature Cocktails Premium Spirits Late Night Energy
